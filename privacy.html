Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information at One Leg Up.

Last updated September 24, 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account, RSVP to an event, or send us a message, we collect the information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, and username. We also keep a record of your event RSVPs and your membership status.

Payments

Membership payments are processed by our payment processor. Your card details go directly to them and never touch our servers. We do not see or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your information to run your membership, manage event RSVPs, and contact you about events and your account. That is all we use it for.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share it with third parties except where needed to run the service, such as processing your payment.

Questions about your data

If you have questions about your personal information, reach out through our contact page and we will answer you.
