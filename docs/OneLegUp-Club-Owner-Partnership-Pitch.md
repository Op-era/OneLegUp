# One Leg Up — Club Owner Partnership

---

## Who We Are

One Leg Up hosts elegant lifestyle parties in the Fresno and Central Valley area. Our website, [onelegup.club](https://onelegup.club), is the membership and RSVP hub where guests discover upcoming nights out, RSVP, and stay connected with the local lifestyle community.

We know that great evenings often start with trusted hosts and well-run clubs. This partnership program is designed to help those hosts reach the right audience without adding cost or complexity.

---

## The Offer

We are inviting selected club owners and hosts in the Fresno area to join as **Club Owner partners** with a **free Club Owner account**.

There is no monthly fee for partner club owners. This is a complimentary pilot for hosts we believe align with the tone and standards of the One Leg Up community.

---

## What You Get

- **Club Owner Panel** — A dedicated dashboard where you publish your own event listings on onelegup.club.
- **Party posters and flyers** — Upload artwork for each event so it appears as an upcoming party on the public site.
- **Built-in audience** — Reach guests who are already browsing One Leg Up when they plan their next night out.
- **No monthly fee** — Partner club owner accounts are free for the duration of this program.

Your listings appear alongside One Leg Up events, giving your club visibility with an audience that is already interested in lifestyle social events in the region.

---

## How It Works

1. **Invitation** — Shane (One Leg Up admin) sends you a personal email invitation to join as a Club Owner partner.
2. **Set your password** — Our email system (Resend) delivers a secure set-password link so you can activate your account.
3. **Log in** — You sign in to the **Club Owner Panel** on onelegup.club.
4. **Create your listing** — Add your party with a title, date, description, and poster or flyer image.
5. **Go live** — Your event listing publishes on the public site for guests to discover.

The process is straightforward. Most hosts can publish their first listing in a few minutes after accepting the invitation.

---

## What We Ask in Return

We keep the platform welcoming, accurate, and respectful. As a partner, we ask that you:

- Publish **accurate listings** with clear dates, locations (as appropriate), dress codes, and entry details.
- Use **tasteful posters and flyers** that reflect well on your club and on the One Leg Up community.
- **Respect guest privacy and consent culture** — the standards our members expect at every event we promote.
- **Avoid spam** — no bulk messaging, misleading promotions, or repeated low-quality posts.

We review partner listings and reserve the right to pause or remove accounts that do not meet these expectations.
