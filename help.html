Cancel Your Membership

You can cancel anytime — no calls, no hoops. Your access stays active through the end of your current billing period.

  • 1
    Log in to your account. If you're not logged in, click "My Dashboard" above and sign in first.
  • 2
    Click the "Cancel Membership" button below (or find it on your Dashboard).
  • 3
    You'll be taken to the secure Stripe billing portal where you can cancel in one click. No forms, no waiting.
  • 4
    After canceling, your membership stays active until the end of the billing cycle you already paid for.
You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure portal — we never store your card details.
